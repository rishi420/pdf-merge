How to turn photos into a PDF on iPhone

Schools, offices and visa forms often ask for "one PDF," not a pile of photos. Here is how to make one on your iPhone.

Updated November 2026

Free way: the Print trick in Photos

  1. Open Photos, tap Select, and tap the photos you want.
  2. Tap the Share button, scroll down and tap Print.
  3. In the print preview, open the share menu at the top and choose Save to Files (on some iOS versions, spread two fingers on the preview first to open it as a PDF).

This works, but each photo keeps its original size, so pages can look uneven, and there is no easy way to reorder them.

Faster way: PDF Merge

  1. Open PDF Merge and add photos from your library. You can also add existing PDFs or scan a page with the camera.
  2. Drag the pages into order and remove any you don't want.
  3. Save as one PDF and share it.

It also works the other way: you can turn PDF pages back into images when someone asks for a JPG.

Tip: photo or scan?

If the photo is of paper, such as a receipt or a form, scan it instead. A scan straightens the page and crops the edges, so it looks sharper and the file is smaller. See how to scan a document to PDF.

Common questions

Can I make a PDF from JPG, PNG and HEIC photos?

Yes. iPhone photos are usually HEIC, and screenshots are PNG. All of them can go into the same PDF.

Will my photos be uploaded anywhere?

No. PDF Merge works on your device. Your photos are only shared if you choose to share the finished file.
